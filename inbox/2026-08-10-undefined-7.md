# 

Follow-up scoping decisions on the QMS Template Tagging Assistant after reviewing the Affordable Storage sample lease. Dropped from scope entirely: spelled-out/written dollar amounts (no tag chase), lease-term-in-months (leases are effectively month-to-month in practice, not worth solving). Approved as the v1 default: the "any fix requiring rewriting non-blank prose, not just filling a blank, always gets flagged for confirmation regardless of tag-choice confidence" rule from the composite-field discussion. New rule locked: when a document has multiple phone-type blanks (Home/Work/Cell) and only one QMS tag exists (e.phone), map the Cell blank to e.phone and leave Home/Work untagged/no-match by design, not an oversight -- same default when there's a single generic "Phone#" blank with no H/W/C breakdown. Vehicle/RV/boat tags: confirmed a vi.* (Vehicle Information) family exists (referenced under m.*/l.* and under Transfer's t.nl.*/t.ol.*) but no individual field-level key names (plate/VIN/make/model/color) have been found anywhere transcribed -- deliberately did NOT fabricate plausible-looking keys and insert them into client_ops.qms_tag, since a wrong guessed key would silently fail to resolve (or print literally) in a real client document; added to Boris's live-QMS-tooltip verification list rather than guessed. DOB and business/company tags remain open, Boris researching independently. PDF-as-source-document fidelity: discussed honestly -- PDF->DOCX conversion (LibreOffice free/local vs. commercial converters like Adobe/Aspose) reliably loses fidelity specifically on page borders/corner marks, shaded/grey text backgrounds, and tab-aligned-rather-than-tabled layouts, because a PDF retains no structural (table/frame/border) information for a converter to reconstruct -- this is inherent to the format, not a Rust-specific limitation. Rasterizing the PDF to an image with overlaid text was ruled out outright as a fidelity workaround since the result wouldn't be a real Word document QMS's merge engine could populate. Recommendation for whenever PDF support is actually scoped (not yet, v1 stays docx-only per earlier agreement): try free local conversion first but require a mandatory human visual-diff/proofread step before any PDF-sourced output ships, never trust the conversion alone.
