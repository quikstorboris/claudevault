# 

Analyzed a real sample lease (Affordable Storage, "FM-Lease---Dec-2025.pdf", a blank fill-in-the-blank template, not a filled example) as a concrete stress test for the QMS Template Tagging Assistant's field-recognition design. Key findings, to inform Phase 2 design before building: (1) Blanks split into two recognition classes -- form-field style (label printed immediately next to the blank, e.g. "Name___", "Zip___") is tractable via label-proximity rules; prose-embedded style (label is a sentence pattern, e.g. "Unit/Space number ___", "hereinafter called 'Tenant'") is also tractable but needs a growing library of known sentence-pattern templates, not one generic rule -- brittle across different lessors' phrasing by nature, improves organically as more documents are read, same shape as the tag catalog's own growth. (2) Composite multi-blank fields are real, not hypothetical: the lease-start date is split into three blanks (day/month/year) that only mean something as a group; the rent amount is split into a spelled-out-words blank and a numeric $ blank for the same value. Proposed model: any fix requiring rewriting non-blank prose (not just filling a blank), such as collapsing the three date blanks into one {{m.indate}} tag, must always be flagged for OM confirmation regardless of how confident the tag choice is -- this is a different risk axis (how much of the document is safe to touch) than tag-selection ambiguity, and needs its own always-flag rule. (3) This document independently reproduces the exact open d.now/d.nowlong-vs-m.indate ambiguity already logged in the vault's open-questions note -- real-world confirmation it's a recurring issue, not a one-off. (4) Candidate tag gaps found by cross-referencing this document's fields against everything transcribed so far (unconfirmed, Boris to verify against live QMS tooltips): no home/work/cell phone breakdown (only generic e.phone documented), no date-of-birth tag, no lease-term-in-months tag (possibly because QMS doesn't track this at all, defaults to month-to-month), and no tags anywhere for a business tenant's own company name/address/authorized-signer-name/signature (the c.* Company family refers to the self-storage operator, e.g. "Affordable Storage" itself, not a renting business entity -- a different concept). Likely real matches found: T.D.L./D.O.B. field maps to existing e.dlnum/e.dlstate; Boat/Motor/VIN/license-plate fields likely map to the vi.* (Vehicle Information) family already referenced in the tag notes. Also identified a new field category: some blanks (Check#, Cash, "Access only:", staff "By___" signature, Project, Rate) read as staff/office-use fields rather than tenant-facing merge content, and may be legitimately out of scope for tagging entirely rather than missing tags. (5) Scoping decision flagged, not yet resolved: source documents will need to support both .docx and PDF, but PDF is a materially harder and different problem for the OUTPUT side specifically (deliverable must always be Word; a .docx source can be edited in place and preserve layout, a PDF source cannot -- would need lossy PDF-to-Word conversion or a rebuild-from-scratch approach). Recommended treating .docx as the real v1 target and deciding what "support PDF" should even mean as a separate follow-on decision, not assuming both get identical treatment.
